Intelligence
Intelligence in AI refers to the ability of machines (computers or software) to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence
This includes:
- Understanding the language
- Learning
- reasoning
- making decisions
- problem solving

Types of AI intelligence

1. Narrow AI(weak AI)
- Designed for specific tasks
- Example: Voice assistants, recommendation systems
2. General AI(not fully developed yet)
- Can perform any intellectual task like a human
3. Super AI
- Hpothetical AI that surpasses human intelligence

Intelligent Behavior
- Intelligence is the capacity, while intelligent behaviour is the expression of that capacity
- To know if a system is intelligent, it is done by observing its intelligent behaviour
- Intelligence = ability
- Intelligent behaviour = result of that ability
